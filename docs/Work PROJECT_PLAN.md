# BusinessPilot AI

## Vision
Build an AI business assistant that helps businesses automate customer support, appointments, product recommendations, and reporting.

## Target Customers
- Restaurants
- Salons
- Schools
- Hotels
- Hospitals
- Real Estate Agencies
- Churches
- Online Stores

## Version 1
- Answer customer questions
- Business information
- Product information

## Version 2
- Appointment booking
- PDF reading
- Voice messages
- Daily reports
- Customer memory

## Future Versions
- AI phone calls
- Website chatbot
- Inventory management
- Payment integration
- Staff dashboard
- Multi-language support
